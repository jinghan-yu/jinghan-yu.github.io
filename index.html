About me

I am a PhD student at Fudan University, co-advised by Prof. Tao Xie and Prof. Wei Yang.

My research interests include multimodal large language models, diffusion models, and intelligent agents, including GUI agents and agent operating systems.

I received my MSc in Professional Computer Science from Simon Fraser University and my BSc in Computer Science from the University of Alberta.
